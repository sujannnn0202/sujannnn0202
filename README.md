## Featured Projects

### AeroCapture: In-Wheel Triboelectric Particle Trap
*Team of 4 | CleanTech 2026 | Concept design*

A zero-power trap mounted behind the brake caliper that captures brake and tire dust at the source, before it reaches the air.

- **Contact charging:** dust is charged by copper and PTFE walls, with no high-voltage supply
- **Velocity drop:** an expansion chamber slows the air so electrostatic force can beat drag
- **Collection:** charged particles deposit on grounded plates and fall into a detachable sump box
- **Maintenance:** an optical sensor alerts the driver when the box is about 90% full
- **Design goals:** no aerodynamic drag, preserved brake-rotor cooling, hidden inside the wheel barrel

Status: concept stage. Prototype build and capture-efficiency testing are the next steps.

### UseItUp: Stop Wasting Food at Home
*Web app | HTML, CSS, JavaScript | Built with AI-assisted workflows*

A kitchen tool that helps households use food before it spoils.

- Add what you bought and when; the app estimates the expiry date automatically
- Separate views for the fridge, items kept outside, and expired items
- Suggests dishes that use the fastest-expiring foods first, with an option to add your own dishes
- Tracks a food-saving score in rupees: money saved by using food in time versus money wasted
- Multilingual interface, including Tamil

Live demo: [UseItUp](https://claude.ai/artifact/1J75fpPj6zaqVPLkQPHxZf)
